## 1\. Core Identity

* **Agency Name:** Kalpana Media (Sanskrit: कल्पना \- Imagination/Creation).  
* **Ethos:** A "mom-and-pop" digital workshop rather than a corporate factory. Trusted, personal, and aligned.  
* **Slogan:** “Digital marketing and website support for conscious small businesses.”

## 2\. Mission Statement

We exist to serve founders who are doing meaningful work — often alone — and need a trusted partner to help with websites, social media, and digital presence without pressure, overwhelm, or aggressive sales tactics.

We believe marketing can be honest, human, and aligned with your values — and that your work deserves to be seen without sacrificing your well-being. We are here to simplify the technical side of marketing so our clients can stay focused on what they do best: serving their communities and making a positive impact.

## 3\. Basic Service Ethos

* **Radical Honesty:** Be super honest about our experience. Humble.  
* **Reliability:** Under-promise, over-deliver. Do great for them.  
* **Authenticity:** No guarantees to go viral or reach 6 figures. No gimmicks.  
* **Value-First:** Our prices are low because we are just starting out. We have experience, and we want to provide real value that is aligned with what we can realistically offer.

## 4\. Customer Avatar

* **The Overwhelmed Creative:** "Is the fast pace of technology draining your energy and attention?"  
* **The Trend-Averse:** "Are you a creative type who gets overwhelmed by the fast pace of trends, AI, and business operations?"  
* **The Manual Founder:** "I wish someone could take care of the operations and maintain the basics for me."  
* **Core Desires:** Time, money, energy, meaning, and serving others.

## 5\. Service Offerings

* **The Baseline Support:** Managing the baseline operations at an affordable price.  
* **Website, Newsletter, & Blog:** Custom sites or maintaining existing inf that serve as an "Oasis" for the brand.  
* **Social Media:** Management as a service (not used for our own outreach, but offered to clients).  
* **Strategic Funnels:** Lead generation, sales/acquisition, and nurturing fans.  
* **VA Services:** General assistant services to be discussed as a side offering on a per-client basis.
