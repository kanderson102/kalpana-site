# Brand Style Guide: Kalpana Media
**Ethos:** A digital workshop, not a factory. Human, aligned, and conscious.

---

## 1. Color Palette
The brand utilizes a "Coastal & Candy" palette. It relies on a clean white foundation with professional blues and a high-energy accent for conversion.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Background** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Main canvas, sections, and card bodies. |
| **Primary Text** | Purple Navy | `#46538B` | Body copy, secondary headers, and UI borders. |
| **Primary Header** | Steel Blue | `#4796AE` | H1 titles, navigation links, and primary icons. |
| **Secondary/UI** | Sea Foam Green | `#AFE0C8` | Success states, card borders, and subtle accents. |
| **Call to Action** | Candy Pink | `#ED7672` | Buttons, active links, and urgent notifications. |
| **High Contrast** | Black | `#000000` | Deepest shadows or maximum emphasis text. |

---

## 2. Typography Strategy

### **Headings**
* **H1 (Hero/Impact):** Steel Blue (`#4796AE`). Used for the main mission statement and hero section.
* **H2 - H4 (Sub-headers):** Purple Navy (`#46538B`). Used for service titles, feature blocks, and section names.

### **Body Copy**
* **Primary Text:** Purple Navy (`#46538B`). This is the default for all paragraph text to ensure the site feels sophisticated rather than clinical.
* **Muted Text:** A 70% opacity version of Purple Navy for less important metadata or labels.

### **Link & Button Text**
* **Active Links:** Candy Pink (`#ED7672`).
* **Button Label:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) on a solid Candy Pink background.

---

## 3. UI Component Specifications

### **Navigation Bar**
- **Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or a very thin Steel Blue (`#4796AE`) top border.
- **Links:** Purple Navy (`#46538B`) with a Candy Pink (`#ED7672`) hover state.

### **Content Cards**
- **Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Border:** 1px solid Sea Foam Green (`#AFE0C8`).
- **Corner Radius:** 8px (Soft/Workshop feel).
- **Shadow:** Subtle 10% opacity Purple Navy shadow for depth.

### **Buttons**
- **Primary:** Background `#ED7672` (Candy Pink), Text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Secondary (Ghost):** Border 2px `#4796AE` (Steel Blue), Text `#4796AE`.

### **Footer**
- **Background:** Steel Blue (`#4796AE`) or Purple Navy (`#46538B`).
- **Text:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or light Sea Foam Green (`#AFE0C8`).

---

## 4. Visual Principles
1. **The 60-30-10 Rule:** Use White for 60% of the space, Blue/Navy tones for 30% of the structural elements, and Candy Pink for the final 10% (interactivity).
2. **Breathing Room:** Maintain high padding (minimum 64px) between major vertical sections to reflect a "conscious" and "non-aggressive" user experience.
3. **Hierarchy:** Direct the eye to the Steel Blue headers first, then the Purple Navy body text, and finally the Candy Pink action items.
