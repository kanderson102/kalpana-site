Welcome to **The Library**. This is our collection of field notes, thoughts, and resources for conscious entrepreneurs.

## Why "The Library"?

In a world of infinite scrolling feeds and ephemeral content, a library represents something different:
*   Depth over breadth
*   Intention over attention-seeking
*   Timelessness over trends

We want this space to be a resource you can return to when you need clarity, inspiration, or practical advice on navigating the digital landscape without losing your soul.

### What to Expect

We'll be sharing thoughts on:
1.  **Digital Minimalism:** How to use technology without it using you.
2.  **Slow Marketing:** Building relationships rather than just funnels.
3.  **Technical Zen:** Simplifying your stack for peace of mind.

Stay tuned.
